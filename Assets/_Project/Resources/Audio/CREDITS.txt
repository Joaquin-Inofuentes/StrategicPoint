Audio replacements added for Strategic Point (prototype, not for distribution).

- Sfx/FootstepGrass, Sfx/FootstepConcrete, Sfx/Hit, Sfx/VehicleHit, Sfx/ImpactMetal,
  Sfx/ImpactDirt, Sfx/Order, Sfx/Swap, Sfx/EmptyClick:
  Kenney "Impact Sounds" + "Interface Sounds" packs. CC0 (public domain). https://kenney.nl

- Sfx/Shot_Rifle, Sfx/Shot_Pistol, Sfx/Shot_Heavy, Sfx/Shoot:
  Real gunshot recordings (Marlin 336 rifle, 1911 pistol, Mossberg + Charles Daly
  shotguns), trimmed to a single shot from "The Free Firearm Sound Library"
  ("Prepared SFX Library"), picked out of 20 analyzed candidates -- see
  Assets/_Project/Audio_GunshotCandidates/ for the full shortlist. CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

- Sfx/Death, Sfx/Wounded:
  Male yell/grunt/scream recordings. CC0 / OGA-BY 3.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds

- Sfx/UiHover, Sfx/UiClick:
  Kenney "Interface Sounds" pack (tick_*, select_*). CC0. https://kenney.nl

- Sfx/OrderBark:
  Individual words ("Accepted", "Positive", "Yes", "Ready") from AAron - Aussie
  Announcer voicepack by Azzurro. Free, "no credit required but appreciated".
  https://azzurrobatic.itch.io/aaron

- Music/Calm.mp3 ("Thinking Music") and Music/Action.mp3 ("Volatile Reaction"):
  Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com). Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 3.0.
  http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

- Heli/RotorReal_Freesound_qubodup.wav (CC0/public domain, uploader qubodup):
  "Helicopter Rotor Loop", a real military helicopter recording extracted from a
  US Government agency video (https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/187681/).
  Downloaded from Freesound's public preview CDN (the original .flac needs a
  Freesound login to download; the hq preview stream does not). Trimmed to cut the
  silent edges and given a 12 ms fade on each end so the loop doesn't click. Used
  by the extraction helicopter of the main mission.
  (Ronda 13 replaced the earlier Heli/Chinook_flying_over_Greenwich.ogg, a real
  Wikimedia Commons recording of a Chinook over Greenwich, CC0/uploader JoBrodie --
  correct but a single 62 s documentary take, not built to loop cleanly. Still CC0;
  kept out of the build only because this one loops better.)

- Sfx/Shot_Rocket/Shot_Rocket_RPG.wav (CC0, uploader klangfabrik):
  "rpg launcher three shots.wav", a real exterior recording of an RPG (rocket
  propelled grenade) launcher/anti-tank weapon firing
  (https://freesound.org/people/klangfabrik/sounds/249298/). Downloaded from
  Freesound's public preview CDN (same method as the helicopter rotor: the
  hq preview stream needs no login). The source has three shots; kept only
  the first (~1.1 s, launch crack + its natural echo) with a 5 ms fade-in and
  a 60 ms fade-out so the cut doesn't click. Used by WeaponKind.Rocket
  (GenericSfx.GetWeaponShot picks it up automatically from Resources/Audio/
  Sfx/Shot_Rocket -- no code change needed).

- Music/Tension.wav: generated procedurally by a script (drone + heartbeat + strings + clock);
  no third-party material.

- Sfx/Explosion (explosionCrunch_000..004.ogg):
  Kenney "Sci-fi Sounds" pack. CC0 (public domain). https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds

- Sfx/GrenadeBounce (impactMetal_light_000..002.ogg):
  Kenney "Impact Sounds" pack (same pack already used for Sfx/ImpactMetal). CC0
  (public domain). https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

- Barks en espanol ("Cubranse!"/"Granada!"/"Muevanse!"): buscados en freesound.org
  (filtrando CC0) y en itch.io (packs de voces militares). No se encontro nada
  utilizable con licencia clara: freesound no tiene resultados decentes para esos
  terminos en espanol, y el unico pack de voz de soldado en espanol encontrado
  ("Soldier Pack Voice - Spanish" de Alex, itch.io) es gratis mediante "name your
  own price" pero NO declara licencia (ni CC0 ni CC-BY), asi que se descarto por
  licencia incierta -- misma limitacion documentada para OrderBark (que quedo en
  ingles). Estos barks siguen sin clip real: caen al tono sintetico generado por
  codigo (ver GenericSfx/SfxSintetico) hasta que aparezca una fuente CC0 confiable.
